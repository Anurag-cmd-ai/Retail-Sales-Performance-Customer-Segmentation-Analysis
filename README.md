## Retail-Sales-Performance-Customer-Segmentation-Analysis
An end-to-end data analytics project on a real-world retail sales dataset: cleaned the data, explored it, answered business questions in SQL, segment customers with RFM, and presented everything in a Power BI dashboard.

## Objective
Find out where revenue comes from, where profit is lost, and which customers matter most, and turn the answers into clear actions for the business.

# Scope
1. Data cleaning: fix types, names and formats so the data is analysis-ready
2. Exploratory analysis (EDA): sales, profit, products, regions and time trends
3. SQL business questions: 12 queries using aggregation, CTEs and window functions
4. Customer segmentation: RFM scoring (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) into 4 segments
5. Dashboard: 3 Power BI pages with KPIs, filters and drill-downs

## Tools
Python (pandas, NumPy)	Cleaning, EDA, RFM scoring
Matplotlib, Seaborn	Charts in the notebooks
MySQL 8	Business-question queries (CTEs, window functions)
Power BI	Interactive dashboard and DAX measures
Jupyter Notebook	Documenting the analysis

## Results

# What the analysis found: 
Area	Result:
1. Growth	Sales grew 29.5% in 2016 and 20.4% in 2017, and profit grew every year
2. Seasonality	Sales peak in September, November and December
3. Discounts	Margin falls from 29.5% with no discount to -77% above 40%, and discounts over 30% lose money on almost every order line
4. Categories	Furniture earns a 2.5% margin against about 17% for Technology and Office Supplies
5 Loss-makers	Tables, Bookcases and Supplies lose money overall, and 16% of products have negative total profit
6. Regions	West has the best margin (14.9%) and Central the weakest (7.9%)
7. States	Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois are big sellers but lose money
8. Customers	Champions are 28% of customers but bring 40% of revenue
9. Churn risk	$785K (34%) of revenue sits with At Risk and Lost customers
    
# Recommendations
1. Cap discounts at 20%
2. Review pricing on Tables, Bookcases, Supplies and Machines
3. Run a win-back campaign for At Risk customers
4. Prepare stock and promotions for September to December
Full findings and SQL answers are in INSIGHTS.md.
