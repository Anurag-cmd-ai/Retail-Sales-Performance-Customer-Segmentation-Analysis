# Retail Sales & Customer Segmentation Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of a real-world retail sales dataset, from data cleaning to SQL insights, RFM customer segmentation and a Power BI dashboard.

---

## Objective

Find out **where revenue comes from, where profit is lost, and which customers matter most**, and turn the answers into clear actions for the business.

## Scope

- **Data cleaning:** fix types, names and formats so the data is analysis-ready
- **Exploratory analysis (EDA):** sales, profit, products, regions and time trends
- **SQL business questions:** 12 queries using aggregation, CTEs and window functions
- **Customer segmentation:** RFM scoring (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) into 4 segments
- **Dashboard:** 3 Power BI pages with KPIs, filters and drill-downs

Not in scope: forecasting and machine-learning models.

### What the analysis found

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| **Growth** | Sales grew 29.5% in 2016 and 20.4% in 2017, and profit grew every year |
| **Seasonality** | Sales peak in September, November and December |
| **Discounts** | Margin falls from 29.5% with no discount to -77% above 40%, and discounts over 30% lose money on almost every order line |
| **Categories** | Furniture earns a 2.5% margin against about 17% for Technology and Office Supplies |
| **Loss-makers** | Tables, Bookcases and Supplies lose money overall, and 16% of products have negative total profit |
| **Regions** | West has the best margin (14.9%) and Central the weakest (7.9%) |
| **States** | Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois are big sellers but lose money |
| **Customers** | Champions are 28% of customers but bring 40% of revenue |
| **Churn risk** | $785K (34%) of revenue sits with At Risk and Lost customers |

### Recommendations

1. Cap discounts at 20%
2. Review pricing on Tables, Bookcases, Supplies and Machines
3. Run a win-back campaign for At Risk customers
4. Prepare stock and promotions for September to December
